What is this timer?

Hey guys! This tool isn't just a timer—it’s a stopwatch, too! It’s perfect for Rubik’s Cube solvers, students preparing for exams, or anyone else who needs to track time. I, Sobhan Rahimkarimi, built this; my assistant, Samiheh, handled the color palette—using a color picker tool—and wrote the CSS. In short, I did the heavy lifting! If you want to play some background music, just click anywhere on the page—essentially interacting with the site—and some relaxing music will start playing.
